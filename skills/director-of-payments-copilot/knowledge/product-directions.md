# Director of Payments Copilot Ideas

The repo should not become a bag of shallow payment runbooks. The stronger idea
is one deep copilot that understands the Director of Payments operating model and
can move between diagnostics, cadence, executive narrative, and strategy.

## Core Product

Turn the playbook into an interactive operating partner for payment leaders.

High-value asks:

- "What should I review Monday morning?"
- "Build my WBR agenda from this metric pack."
- "Explain this payment performance change to my CFO."
- "What belongs in the MBR vs the QBR?"
- "Pressure-test my annual plan."
- "Draft a three-year payment strategy from these constraints."

## Deep Modes

### Operating Cadence

Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual, and three-year workflows stay in one
skill so the agent understands how each artifact rolls into the next.

### Diagnostic Lens

The copilot should diagnose payment performance, but not as a separate
authorization-rate skill. Diagnosis is one layer of the broader leadership
workflow: what changed, what is controllable, who needs to know, and what the
team should do next.

### Executive Narrative

The copilot should translate raw payment movement into business language:
revenue protected, cost reduced, customer experience improved, and operational
risk lowered.

### Strategy Workbench

The copilot should help build annual plans and three-year strategies, including
processor redundancy, payment data ownership, AI interface readiness, and funded
initiative themes.

## Demo Shape

The demo should show one conversation moving up the altitude stack:

1. Start with a weekly metric snapshot.
2. Produce a WBR agenda.
3. Convert the same data into an executive MBR narrative.
4. Identify the strategic theme for QBR.
5. Add the theme to annual planning.
6. Update a three-year payment operating tenet.
