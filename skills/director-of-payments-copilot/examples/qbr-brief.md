# Example QBR Brief

## Goal

Move payments from a supporting slide to a dedicated strategic discussion.

## Executive Frame

Payments performance affects revenue capture, cost of acceptance, and customer
experience. This quarter's review should show what changed, what the team can
control, and which investments accelerate the next step-change.

## Suggested Outline

1. Payment performance snapshot
   - payment success rate
   - authorization rate
   - recovered orders
   - cost of payments
   - customer contacts related to payment failure

2. What changed this quarter
   - largest movement by payment method, processor, region, or decline reason
   - launch, test, fraud, or support events that influenced the numbers

3. What we controlled
   - retry strategy
   - account updater adoption
   - network-token usage
   - routing optimization
   - customer fix-up cadence

4. What we need from leadership
   - engineering capacity
   - processor redundancy investment
   - data access
   - product roadmap support

5. Strategic bets
   - reduce single-processor risk
   - protect merchant-owned payment data
   - use AI for anomaly detection, trend review, and payments operating workflow
