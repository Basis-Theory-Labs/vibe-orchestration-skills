# Executive Narrative Template

Use this when the output needs to be consumed by finance, executives, or a QBR
audience.

## Structure

### Headline

One sentence that connects payment movement to business impact.

### What Changed

Name the metric movement and affected segment. Avoid payment jargon unless it is
necessary.

### Why It Matters

Translate into:

- revenue protected or lost
- cost maintained or reduced
- customer experience improved or degraded
- risk reduced or introduced

### What Is Controllable

Separate internal levers from external behavior. Be explicit about what the
payments team can and cannot control.

### What We Are Doing

List actions with owners. Use action verbs.

### Decision Or Ask

Only include an ask when the audience can actually help.

## Example Tone

```text
Payment success softened this week, concentrated in LATAM card traffic. The
early signal points to a regional issuer-behavior shift plus a possible checkout
timeout introduced in Thursday's release. We are separating external decline
behavior from internal checkout impact before changing routing or retry logic.
```
