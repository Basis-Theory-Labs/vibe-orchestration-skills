Use this for annual planning and three-year strategy.


## Annual Planning Themes

Group work into themes. Each theme needs an output value.

### Theme

- Name:
- Business outcome:
- Output value: revenue, cost, CX, or risk
- Why now:
- Current evidence:
- Required owners:
- Dependencies:
- Executive ask:
- Success metric:

## Three-Year Strategy Tenets

Tenets should be memorable enough that every payment team member can articulate,
defend, and apply them to a company goal.

Potential tenets:

- Every high-scale merchant should have processor redundancy.
- Payment data ownership should remain with the merchant or platform.
- AI should become a primary interface for payment operations.
- Payment performance should have a dedicated executive operating cadence.
- Recovery and customer fix-up should be treated as product surfaces, not only
  back-office workflows.

## Output

Return:

- executive summary
- 3-5 annual themes
- each theme's output value
- required investments
- sequencing
- open decisions
- three-year operating tenets
