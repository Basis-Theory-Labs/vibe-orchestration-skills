# Director of Payments Playbook Post Draft

Most payments content talks about optimization tactics.

Auth rates. Retries. Tokenization. Routing. Fraud.

All important.

But the harder problem for a Director of Payments is organizational:

How do you make payments visible before something breaks?

Because when payments work, nobody notices.
When payments fail, everyone looks at you.

That is why Kevin Mayes put together the Director of Payments Playbook.

It is less "here are some metrics" and more "here is the operating cadence":

- what to check daily
- how to run a useful WBR
- how to frame payment outcomes for executives
- how to move from being a slide in someone else's QBR to owning the room
- where AI starts taking over anomaly detection, trend review, and alerts

The part I keep coming back to:

AI probably does not replace the payment leader.
It becomes the interface that helps them see what changed, what matters, and
what needs action.

So I got curious whether a payments playbook should just be a PDF, or whether it
should also be a skill you can talk to.

We are experimenting with the second version:

`director-of-payments-copilot`

A lightweight payment-ops skill that can help turn metrics into WBR agendas,
QBR narratives, annual planning themes, and payment strategy prompts.
