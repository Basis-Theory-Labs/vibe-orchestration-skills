# Payment Domain Model

Use this lightweight model across skills so the repo speaks one payment language.

## Operating Cadence

- Daily operations - catch failures before they compound.
- Weekly business review - turn changes into action and owners.
- Monthly business review - show trends, programs, problems, and cost impact.
- Quarterly business review - elevate payments into strategic planning.
- Annual planning - group initiatives by revenue, cost, and customer-experience
  impact.
- Three-year strategy - define the payment organization's desired future state.

## Core Metrics

Input metrics are levers the payment team can influence:

- authorization rate
- retry success rate
- customer fix-up rate
- recovery rate
- account updater adoption
- network-token usage
- routing optimization
- payment-method mix

Output metrics are business results:

- net sales volume
- payment success rate
- recovered orders
- cost of payments
- transaction volume
- customer contacts

## Analysis Dimensions

Cut payment data by:

- payment method
- acquirer or processor
- region
- issuer or BIN range when available
- decline reason
- retry attempt
- customer communication channel

## Business Framing

Tie payment work to:

- revenue protected
- cost reduced
- customer experience improved
- risk reduced
- roadmap or engineering tradeoffs clarified
