# Payment Skill Ideas

## Director of Payments Copilot

Turn the playbook into an interactive payment-ops assistant.

Example asks:

- "What should I review Monday morning?"
- "Build my WBR agenda."
- "Explain this auth-rate drop to my CFO."
- "What should go in the QBR?"
- "Draft annual planning themes from these payment initiatives."

## WBR Generator

Paste weekly metrics and get:

- what changed
- what is controllable
- what is external
- who owns each follow-up
- executive-ready summary

## Authorization-Rate Diagnosis

Investigate rate movement by:

- payment method
- processor
- region
- issuer
- raw decline reason
- customer fix-up and retry behavior

## Payment Incident Commander

Run an incident with:

- severity
- impact
- owner map
- mitigation checklist
- internal comms draft
- recovery criteria

## QBR Generator

Turn raw payment metrics and initiatives into:

- exec narrative
- cost/revenue/CX framing
- asks from leadership
- strategic roadmap themes

## PSP Orchestration

Generate PSP mappings, SDKs, and demos from declarative payment integration
specs.
